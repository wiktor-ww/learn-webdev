What is it really..?

Web development is the process of designing, developing and maintaining websites and web apps. Web development encompasses several different fields, most commonly referring to the programming of websites. Front-end development is the act of developing the user interface and client-side code, while back-end development focuses on the infrastructure behind a website, mainly server-side code.

The Developers

  • Frontend developers are responsible for creating the website and bringing the designers’ vision to life.
  • Designers are responsible for creating the overall look and feel of the website. They do one of the most important jobs in the entire project, as good design is challenging.
  • Backend developers handle everything behind the scenes and provide the structural backbone of the website. They are responsible for all databases and server-side resources.
