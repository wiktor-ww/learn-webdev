What is it really..?

Web development is the process of designing, developing and maintaining websites and web apps. Web development encompasses several different fields, most commonly referring to the programming of websites. Front-end development is the act of developing the user interface and client-side code, while back-end development focuses on the infrastructure behind a website, mainly server-side code.

The Developers

  • Frontend developers', their task is to create the website and bring the designers' design to life
  • The designers' task is to design the whole design of the website. They are doing one of the most important job in the whole project (design is hard)
  • The backend developers' are the whole back scene and the whole skeleton of the website. They are responsible for all databases and resources at the server-side.
